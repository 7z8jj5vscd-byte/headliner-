# The Eras Life: fact-check report

This is a review of the game's dates and figures against the author's own knowledge. It is **not** a check against live sources. It marks what is solid, what is approximate, and what needs a real source before anyone relies on it. The game says in its README and interface that real figures are approximate.

Levels:
- **Solid**: widely documented, and the game's value matches the commonly reported one.
- **Approximate**: the event is real, but the exact date, number or wording may be off by a little.
- **Needs a source**: plausible, but should be checked before it is relied on.

## Checked against live sources (November 2026)

- **Showgirl's first week: confirmed.** About 4.002 million units (3.479 million in album sales), the biggest US first week since Luminate tracking began in 1991. Sources: [Variety](https://variety.com/2025/music/news/taylor-swifts-the-life-of-a-showgirl-charts-1236568956), [Music Week](https://www.musicweek.com/labels/read/taylor-swift-breaks-us-records-as-the-life-of-a-showgirl-tops-5-5m-global-album-units-in-week-one/092886).
- **Showgirl's weeks at No. 1: at least 7.** It spent at least its first seven weeks at No. 1 in late 2025, and the latest results found did not say whether the run continued. The game now uses 7. Source: [WDEF](https://www.wdef.com/?p=2266425).
- **TTPD's weeks at No. 1: 15, confirmed (as of 2024).** Twelve straight weeks, then a return to No. 1. Sources: [Billboard](https://ca.billboard.com/music/chart-beat/taylor-swift-tortured-poets-department-14th-week-number-one-billboard-200-chart-1235750808/), [WDEF](https://www.wdef.com/?p=1792544).
- **Midnights' weeks at No. 1: 6, confirmed (as of early 2024).** Source: [Guinness World Records](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/636372-most-cumulative-weeks-at-no-1-on-us-albums-chart-solo-female).
- **The July 3, 2026 wedding: reported, not confirmed in the sources found.** Reports cited save-the-date invitations for New York City on July 3, 2026, after earlier reports of June 13 in Rhode Island. The couple had not confirmed it in those reports. The card stays, following the design report, and its note now says "reported." Sources: [Marie Claire UK](https://www.marieclaire.co.uk/entertainment/celebrity-news/taylor-swift-wedding-date-revealed), [Newcastle Herald](https://www.newcastleherald.com.au/story/9222138/taylor-swifts-wedding-date-and-location-revealed).

## Second live check (November 2026)

- **Songwriters Hall of Fame: confirmed.** Inducted June 11, 2026 in New York, in her first year of eligibility. The card's date and note now match. Sources: [Variety](https://au.variety.com/2026/music/global/taylor-swift-inducted-songwriters-hall-of-fame-32184/), [American Songwriter](https://americansongwriter.com/taylor-swift-inducted-into-songwriters-hall-of-fame-on-her-first-year-of-eligibility).
- **iHeartRadio Music Awards: confirmed.** Showgirl won Pop Album of the Year and Album of the Year on March 26, 2026, among seven wins. Sources: [Variety](https://au.variety.com/2026/music/global/taylor-swift-most-wins-at-iheartradio-music-awards-2026-34694/), [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/music/music-news/2026-iheartradio-music-awards-winners-list-1236548440/).
- **Destiny's Child's 1998 debut: corrected.** It entered the Billboard 200 at about No. 69 and peaked at No. 67. The game now debuts it at No. 69. No reliable first-week sales figure was found, so the game's 15K stays an estimate. Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Destiny%27s_Child_(album)).
- **Mayhem: debut confirmed.** No. 1 with 219,000 units. Total weeks at No. 1 were not found; the game uses one. Source: [NPR via WBHM](https://wbhm.org/npr-story/lady-gagas-mayhem-shakes-up-the-charts-at-no-1).

Still needing a source: the $26 million wedding donations, the Encore release (September 25, 2026) and the October 2026 wedding photos; the Met Gala and Act III card; the Super Bowl LX cameo and Devil Wears Prada 2 songs; weeks at No. 1 for Beyoncé's and Gaga's other albums.

## What changed in this pass

- Brit Awards 2021 card: removed the claim that she was the first woman and the first non-British artist to receive the Global Icon award. The card now only says she received it.
- Beyoncé's and Lady Gaga's albums without a well-known Billboard run now hold No. 1 for one or two weeks after a No. 1 debut, rather than a sales-based guess.
- Tours with no confirmed gross use a clearly labelled rough estimate and do not count toward the Canon Score.

## Taylor Swift

**Solid**
- Release dates of all studio albums and the four Taylor's Version re-records.
- First-week units: Fearless 592K, Speak Now 1.047M, Red 1.208M, 1989 1.287M, Reputation 1.238M, Lover 867K, folklore 846K, evermore 329K, Midnights 1.578M, 1989 (TV) 1.653M, TTPD 2.61M. The debut entered the chart at No. 19.
- Albums of the Year: Fearless (2010), 1989 (2016), folklore (2021), Midnights (2024).
- Weeks at No. 1: Fearless 11, Speak Now 6, Red 7, 1989 11, Reputation 4, Lover 1, folklore 8, evermore 4.
- Tour grosses: Red Tour about $150M, 1989 World Tour about $250.7M, Reputation Stadium Tour about $345.7M, Eras Tour about $2.08B over 149 shows.
- Major events: the 2009 VMAs, the 2016 call clip, the 2017 trial, the Republic deal (November 2018), the Ithaca purchase (June 2019), the Shamrock sale (2020), the masters buyback (May 2025), the Ticketmaster collapse (November 2022), the Vienna cancellations (August 2024).
- Hot 100 No. 1s used in the game: "We Are Never Ever Getting Back Together", "Shake It Off", "Blank Space", "Bad Blood", "Look What You Made Me Do", "cardigan", "willow", "All Too Well (10 Minute Version)", "Anti-Hero", "Cruel Summer", "Is It Over Now?", "Fortnight", "The Fate of Ophelia".

**Approximate**
- Childhood and early-career dates (2001 to 2005): Nashville trips, the 76ers anthem, the RCA deal, the move to Hendersonville, the Sony/ATV deal. The years are right; the exact weeks are estimates.
- The Bluebird showcase is set to November 2004 and leaving RCA to 2005, following the design report. Some accounts place leaving RCA in 2004.
- Fearless Tour gross (about $63M) and show count. The Speak Now World Tour (about $123.7M) is solid; its show count is approximate.
- Weeks at No. 1 for Midnights (6) and TTPD (15).
- Hot 100 peaks of the smaller singles (for example "The Story of Us" at 41, "New Romantics" at 46).
- Every in-game week number. Real dates are rounded to the nearest week.

**Needs a source**
- The 2026 cards (iHeartRadio Pop Album of the Year, the Songwriters Hall of Fame induction, the July 3 wedding, $26 million in donations, "Patient Zero" and The Encore on September 25, wedding photos in October). These came from the design report, which said they were checked against reporting at the time.
- Showgirl's first week (about 4.0M) and its weeks at No. 1.
- The estimated fan-base curve. It is a game estimate of reach, not an official count.
- The estimated real ticket prices used to size tours.

## How to do a full fact-check

1. Start with the "Needs a source" lists above.
2. Check each figure against a primary or well-established source (Billboard, Pollstar, the RIAA, official announcements).
3. Fix the values in `data-taylor.js`, `data-beyonce.js`, `data-gaga.js`, `data-looks.js` (single peaks and weeks at No. 1) and `data-events-world.js`.
